---
name: edit-market-video
description: Edit English talking-head market commentary with speech-timed stock and cryptocurrency charts, animated prices, and optional phrase captions. Use when the user wants a chart-first video or a presenter inset synchronized with a market discussion.
---

Start with a timestamped transcript and a list of instruments in spoken order. Make the pacing edit first. Keep a mapping from source frames to edited frames and use the edited timeline for every overlay, caption and audio cut. Find the end of the final spoken sentence before trimming the tail.

For each insert, record the instrument, spoken claim, time window, time zone, currency, data source and observed price. Record a hypothetical level or a speaker's trade as such. Use a dated series for the line. Compute the percentage against an explicit baseline. Distinguish regular closes, pre-market observations, period returns and a move from previous close. If the speaker mentions a threshold, show the observation that supports it while that phrase is audible. A briefly animated counter can otherwise appear to contradict the narration.

Choose the composition to suit the footage. Two useful starting points are these 1280 × 720 layouts:
- Option 1 puts the ticker, a thin divider, price and change at the left of the header. A small presenter frame sits at the upper right, with the plot below.
- Option 2 divides the content width 75:25. At 720p, use a 912 × 632 chart column and a 304 × 632 presenter frame, with a 24 px gutter. Crop the camera around the speaker and check the crop at several points.

Use a restrained neutral surface for charts. Give all date ticks a shared baseline, clip the dot texture below the price curve, and crop coin logos in a circle. Animate the line on a seekable timeline. Hold the settled graphic through the discussion. For fast ticker changes, shorten the reveal so viewers can read the end value. Keep optional English captions phrase-based and below the plot and speaker's mouth.

For a new recording, rebuild the cue list, quote data, camera crop and captions. Build the edit from the supplied footage and its transcript. Preview the shortest cue, the longest hold, a stock-to-crypto transition and the closing line. Check face proportions, small-screen label readability, voice loudness, and audio/video duration in the exported file.

When the speaker compares several assets over one period, use the sibling [comparison skill](../compare-asset-performance/SKILL.md) to render a normalized, speech-timed MP4 insert.
